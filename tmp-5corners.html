Welcome to the 5 Corners Guest Inn

Set in picturesque surroundings in Ballyclare, County Antrim, the Five Corners Guest Inn provides the perfect place to relax and unwind.
The Five Corners is comprised of a bar,  Restaurant, Take 5 Coffee shop and guest rooms to offer overnight accomodation.
