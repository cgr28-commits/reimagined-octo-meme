About Us

Your Home
Away From Home

For over a century, Corr’s Corner Hotel has been at the heart of the local community. More than just a place to stay, we’re a home away from home—where guests become part of the family.

Generations of locals and travellers alike have walked through our doors, finding not just a warm welcome, but a place where friendships are made, stories are shared, and memories are created. As a family-owned hotel, we take pride in our deep roots, offering genuine hospitality that has stood the test of time. Conveniently located just 7 miles from Belfast City Centre, we’re easy to reach from Belfast International Airport, Belfast City Airport, and the ferry ports in Larne and Belfast.

Food & Drink

Food is at the heart of what we do, and it’s why families have been coming back for generations. Whether you’re soaking up the buzz of the Lady R Grill Bar or looking for a quieter meal in Corriander Restaurant, you’ll find something to satisfy every craving.
From our famous chicken kebab to our signature roast dinners, we serve up hearty, homely food made with quality ingredients. And with a full cocktail and bar menu, you’ll never go thirsty.
