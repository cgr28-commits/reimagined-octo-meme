Drive-to-pickup charge

Adds a charge for the road miles between your base and the customer's address, beyond the free miles, to each leg of an airport transfer. It is included in the journey fare (Night & Weekend surcharge and the return discount apply as normal) and is never shown as a separate line. Address-to-address and airport-to-airport journeys are not affected. Works whether or not vehicle MPG is set.

Charge for the drive to the pickup

Example: 4 free miles at £1.00/mile — an address 10 road miles from base adds £6 per leg. Rounded to whole pounds. Use the Profitability tester to check a real address.

Address-to-address drive charge

For journeys with no airport (e.g. Belfast to Glenarm). Charges for the road miles from your base to whichever end of the journey is further away, beyond the free miles, so the price is the same whichever way round it is booked. Included in the journey fare and never shown as a separate line. Separate from the airport setting above.

Charge on address-to-address jobs

Example: 4 free miles at £0.50/mile — a journey whose far end is 29 road miles from base (e.g. Glenarm) adds £13 per leg. Rounded to whole pounds.

Return pricing

Applies only to eligible base vehicle fares. Airport, Express and other fixed charges are not discounted.

Evening / Night / Weekend / Bank Holiday

Christmas pricing enabled

Applies every year to pickups between these dates (inclusive, Europe/London). The highest applicable Christmas, Evening, Night or Weekend percentage applies once per leg.

Evening pricing enabled

Disabled by default. Times use Europe/London; the highest applicable Evening, Night or Weekend percentage applies once per leg.

Night pricing enabled

Night hours apply every day; overlapping Weekend pricing uses the higher rate. Current window: 22:00 to 06:00.

Weekend pricing enabled

Qualifying weekend: Saturday and Sunday, all day (Europe/London). Whole days, not hourly start/end times.

When more than one premium period applies, the highest applicable surcharge is used. Christmas, Evening, Night and Weekend are not added together.

Bank Holidays

Daytime bank holidays are not an extra surcharge. There is no separate Bank Holiday calendar. Saturday and Sunday already qualify as Weekend. A weekday bank-holiday daytime journey is charged at the standard weekday rate unless it also falls inside enabled Evening or Night hours.

A 20% Christmas surcharge applies to pickups from 14 December to 31 December. A 10% Night surcharge applies between 22:00 and 06:00. A 10% Weekend surcharge applies all day Sunday and Saturday. Times are Europe/London. When periods overlap, only the highest surcharge applies to each leg’s base vehicle fare; fixed airport charges are excluded.
