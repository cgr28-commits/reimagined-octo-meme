Belfast ⇄ Dublin Christmas & New Year Taxi

Heading to Dublin for a day of Christmas shopping, the lights and the markets, or bringing friends up to Belfast for the Christmas Market at City Hall? Planning the work Christmas dinner, a festive hotel break or New Year's Eve across the border? Book one private car or minibus for the whole group, door to door in either direction, or travel in comfort with Business Class. Nobody has to drive, nobody has to watch the last train or bus, and the price is fixed and agreed before you pay.

A festive day or night out without the travel hassle

December is the busiest month on the road between Belfast and Dublin. Coaches and trains fill up, and getting home late after a Christmas dinner or a day at the markets can mean a long wait. A pre-booked private taxi collects you at your door, drops you where you want to be and brings you home at the time you choose. It suits Christmas shopping trips, market day trips, office Christmas parties, festive concerts and shows, and family visits over the holidays.

Belfast to Dublin for Christmas

We collect from your Belfast home, hotel or office and take you to your Dublin hotel, restaurant, venue or the city centre for the markets and shops. Book a return and tell us the time and place you want collecting for the journey home, whether that is late afternoon after the shopping or later in the evening after a Christmas dinner.

Dublin to Belfast for Christmas

Coming north for the Belfast Christmas Market at City Hall, a Christmas party or a night in the Cathedral Quarter? We collect you from your Dublin address and drop you at your Belfast hotel or the city centre. Add the return to get everyone home together at the end of the night.

Christmas in Belfast and Dublin

Work Christmas dinners and group bookings

Booking for the office Christmas party? One booking covers the whole group, and a 7-Seater Minibus is offered when available for larger groups. For bigger parties we can arrange more than one vehicle, so just enter everyone travelling in the quote or send us a WhatsApp. The journey is usually around two hours each way, depending on the addresses and traffic, so we plan pickups around your dinner or event time. December dates go quickly, so book as early as you can.

Christmas hotel stays and festive breaks

Staying over for a Christmas break, a festive afternoon tea or a hotel Christmas party? We drop you at the hotel door with your bags and collect you again on the day you check out, so the car stays at home and nobody has to drive. Give us the hotel name and the check-out time when you book the return.

New Year's Eve in Belfast or Dublin

Seeing in the New Year in Dublin or Belfast? Book the journey there and your trip home in advance. New Year's Eve and New Year's Day are among the hardest nights of the year to find a taxi, so a pre-booked car with a fixed price takes the worry out of getting home. Tell us exactly where you want collecting and the time, and we plan around road closures where we can.

Travel in style with Business Class

Make the trip part of the occasion. Business Class is a premium executive car, such as a Mercedes, BMW or Lexus, for up to three passengers. It is a great choice for a client Christmas lunch, a special night out or a festive hotel stay in Dublin or Belfast. Choose Business Class in the quote to see your fixed price.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a taxi from Belfast to Dublin for a Christmas day out?

Yes. Book a private taxi door to door from your Belfast address to Dublin, and add the return so we bring you home at the time you choose. The price is fixed and agreed before you pay.

Is there room for our Christmas shopping on the way home?

Yes. Tell us how many passengers are travelling and roughly how much luggage or shopping you expect when you book. An Estate gives extra boot space, and a 7-Seater Minibus suits a group with plenty of bags when available. Your driver collects you from the shopping street, centre or hotel you choose and drops everything at your door.

Can you take a group to a work Christmas dinner in Dublin or Belfast?

Yes. Enter everyone travelling in the quote. A Saloon or Estate takes up to four passengers, and a 7-Seater Minibus is offered for larger groups when available. For bigger parties message us on WhatsApp and we will arrange more than one vehicle.

Can you take us to a Christmas hotel stay and collect us on check-out day?

Yes. Book a return, enter the hotel as the drop-off, and choose the date and time of your check-out for the journey home. We collect you at the hotel entrance.

Can I book a taxi for New Year's Eve between Belfast and Dublin?

Yes. Book both journeys in advance, as New Year's Eve is one of the busiest nights of the year. Give us the exact collection point and time for the trip home and we confirm a fixed price before you pay.

Can we travel in Business Class for a Christmas night out?

Yes. Business Class is a premium executive car, such as a Mercedes, BMW or Lexus, for up to three passengers. Choose it in the quote to see the fixed price.

Can you collect us late after a Christmas party?

Yes. Tell us the collection time and the exact pickup point, such as the restaurant or hotel entrance, when you book the return. Non-airport pickups include 10 minutes' complimentary waiting from the booked time.

How long does it take between Belfast and Dublin?

Usually around two hours each way, depending on the exact addresses, the time of day and traffic. December traffic in both city centres can be heavier, so we plan pickups with a sensible buffer.

Is the price per person?

No. The fixed price is for the vehicle, not per passenger, so a full car or minibus shares one fare.

Should I book early for December?

Yes. December weekends and the run-up to Christmas are our busiest dates. Book as early as you can, and you can cancel up to 24 hours before your pickup for a full refund if your plans change.

Flying home for Christmas through Dublin Airport?

Use our Dublin Airport to Belfast page. Your driver meets you in Arrivals, we track your flight, and airport pickups include up to 60 minutes' waiting.
