Belfast to Cork Taxi & Private Transfer

Travel from Belfast to Cork in one private vehicle, with your pickup, destination and price agreed in advance. We collect from Belfast homes, hotels and offices, as well as Belfast International and George Best Belfast City Airport, for the journey south to Cork.

One booked journey from Belfast to Cork

A long journey is easier when you have one collection point and one car carrying you and your baggage all the way. There is no change of vehicle in Dublin and no need to organise a separate connection across the city. Whether you are travelling for work, visiting family or starting a stay in Cork, your driver takes you to the address agreed in your booking.

Your Belfast pickup

Give us the full hotel name or street address, including a postcode where available. City-centre hotels, the Cathedral Quarter, the Titanic Quarter and south Belfast can have different collection points and journey times, so use your actual address rather than a general Belfast pin. If your hotel has a separate entrance for vehicles, add that detail when arranging the collection.

Where in Cork are you going?

Cork city centre, a hotel near Kent Station, a business address in Little Island and a home in Douglas are different destinations. Enter the full drop-off address or Eircode so your quote covers the correct journey. If you are travelling farther into County Cork, name that destination too; a Cork city quote should not be used for an onward trip to Cobh, Kinsale or another town.

Allow time for a long cross-border journey

Belfast and Cork are at opposite ends of a substantial road journey. Allow roughly five to six hours, depending on the exact addresses, traffic and any agreed stops. The usual motorway journey passes the Dublin area; your actual route and timing depend on conditions on the day.

Let us know about an appointment, onward connection or planned comfort stop before booking. Your pickup time should leave room for that plan, rather than relying on the shortest mapped driving time.

Arriving at a Belfast airport?

Select Belfast International Airport or George Best Belfast City Airport as your pickup and your exact Cork destination. Eligible airport-to-Cork journeys can show an instant fixed price once your addresses, passengers and suitcases are confirmed. Add the flight number and pickup details when booking. Your collection instructions explain where to meet your driver, and flight monitoring applies where available.

Match the vehicle to your party and baggage

Enter every passenger and suitcase before selecting a vehicle. Saloon suits up to four passengers with up to two standard suitcases. Estate offers extra luggage space for a smaller party with more bags. For a larger group, choose a 7-Seater Minibus when the quote offers one. Availability and capacity restrictions still apply.

If you have oversized baggage or select 5+ suitcases, confirm the capacity with us before booking. Child or booster seats are requested subject to availability.

A clear quote before you book

Enter the exact Belfast pickup and Cork drop-off, then select passengers and suitcases. Confirmed Greater Belfast and eligible Belfast airport pickups can receive an instant online price. Add dates and booking details after viewing the fare.

Have your travel date and time ready for the booking or request stage. A quote is not a confirmed reservation; complete the booking steps after the price and availability have been agreed.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Belfast to Cork?

The fare depends on the exact pickup and drop-off, vehicle and journey details. Use the quote form rather than a single headline fare. Confirmed Greater Belfast and eligible Belfast airport collections can show an instant price once addresses, passengers and suitcases are selected.

How long does Belfast to Cork take?

Plan for roughly five to six hours on the road, depending on the addresses, route, traffic and any agreed stops. The quote tool shows a mapped estimate for your journey. Leave additional time for a flight connection or a time-sensitive appointment.

Can you collect from Belfast International or Belfast City Airport?

Yes. Select the correct airport as the pickup, enter your Cork destination and provide your flight details when booking. Follow the collection instructions sent for that airport and contact your driver as advised.

Which vehicle should I choose for a long-distance transfer?

Enter everyone travelling and all standard suitcases before choosing a vehicle. Saloon and Estate carry up to four passengers, with Estate suited to extra luggage. A 7-Seater Minibus is offered only when available and suitable for the party. Vehicle capacity and availability are checked during the quote.

Can we arrange a comfort stop?

Please tell us before booking if you would like a comfort stop, an additional pickup or a detour. We can confirm the plan and any effect on your quote before you commit. Allow extra time if you have a flight or appointment at the destination.

Is the price per passenger or per vehicle?

Your confirmed transfer price is for the booked vehicle and journey, rather than a separate fare for each passenger. Enter the full party and suitcase count so we can offer a suitable vehicle. The price is agreed before payment.

Can I arrange the journey back as well?

Yes. Choose Return and enter the details for both journeys. Where an eligible online return fare is shown, the existing return-booking discount is applied by the quote tool. A personalised return request is confirmed with you before payment.
