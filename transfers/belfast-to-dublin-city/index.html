Travel between Belfast and Dublin city in one pre-booked private car, door to door. One car from your door to theirs

Getting between Belfast and Dublin by coach or train usually means getting to a station first, then finding a taxi at the other end with your luggage. A private transfer takes you from one address to the other in the same car. It suits city breaks, concerts and matches, business meetings, family visits and anyone travelling with heavy bags, children or a group.

Belfast to Dublin city

We collect from your exact Belfast address: a city-centre hotel, an office, a home in south or north Belfast, or Belfast Grand Central Station. Enter the full Dublin drop-off address or Eircode so the quote covers the right journey. A hotel on St Stephen's Green, an office in the Docklands and the Aviva Stadium or 3Arena are all different drop-off points.

Dublin city to Belfast

We collect from your Dublin hotel, home, office or venue at the time you choose and take you straight to your Belfast door. Give us the full pickup address and any access notes, such as a hotel's side entrance or a venue's pickup area. Flying into Dublin instead? Use our Dublin Airport to Belfast page, where your driver meets you in arrivals.

How long does it take?

Belfast to Dublin city is usually around two hours on the A1/M1, depending on the exact addresses, the time of day and traffic in Dublin. The quote shows a mapped estimate for your journey. If you have a show, match, meeting or onward connection, tell us the time you need to be there and we will plan the pickup with a sensible buffer.

Flying in or out of Dublin Airport?

This page is for Dublin city addresses. For flights, use our Dublin Airport pages: your driver monitors the flight and airport pickups include up to 60 minutes' waiting.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Belfast to Dublin city?

The price depends on the exact pickup and drop-off addresses, the vehicle and the journey time. Enter both addresses in the quote form to see your fixed price. The price is for the car, not per passenger, and it is agreed before you pay.

Can you collect me in Dublin and take me to Belfast?

Yes. Enter your Dublin hotel, home or office as the pickup and your Belfast address as the drop-off. We confirm the collection time and the fixed price before you book.

How long is the journey between Belfast and Dublin?

Usually around two hours, depending on where you start and finish and on traffic, especially in Dublin city. Allow extra time if you need to arrive for a fixed start time.

Is waiting time included?

Yes. City pickups include 10 minutes' complimentary waiting from the booked pickup time. If you are collecting from an event or a busy venue, tell us when booking and we will agree the best pickup point.

Can I book a return journey?

Yes. Choose Return and enter the details for both journeys, for example to Dublin for a concert and back to Belfast the same night. Where an eligible online return fare is shown, the return discount is applied automatically.

What if my plans change?

You can cancel up to 24 hours before your pickup for a full refund. Contact us as early as possible if your times change and we will do our best to move the booking.

Which vehicle should I choose?

Enter everyone travelling and every suitcase before choosing a vehicle. A Saloon suits up to four passengers with standard luggage, an Estate gives extra boot space, and a 7-Seater Minibus is offered for larger groups when available.
