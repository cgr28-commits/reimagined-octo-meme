Arriving by train or coach is only one part of the journey. A pre-booked transfer gives you an agreed pickup time and a price to review before booking, particularly useful when you are travelling with children, several bags or a business appointment ahead.

Arriving in Belfast

Where to meet your driver

Book for a realistic collection time after your train or coach arrival, leaving time to collect your bags and walk through the station. Keep your phone available and follow the meeting instructions agreed for your booking.

Translink lists general drop-off spaces on Boyne Bridge Place alongside a separate public-hire taxi rank. The dedicated drop-off spaces have a maximum stay of 10 minutes. This is a site access limit, not an extension of your booking’s waiting allowance.

A private pre-booked collection does not mean joining the black-taxi rank. Confirm your meeting point with us; access, signs and space availability may affect the lawful place your driver can stop.

Read Translink’s current station access guide ↗

Catching your connection

Travelling to Grand Central Station

Arrange collection from your home, hotel or workplace and select Belfast Grand Central Station as the destination. Your driver can take you to a permitted drop-off point; allow time to walk inside and find your departure.

For an Enterprise train or another rail or coach service, work back from the departure time on your ticket. Include a margin for traffic, luggage and boarding rather than using the service departure as your taxi pickup time.

Check the station name carefully. Grand Central, Lanyon Place and Grand Central Hotel are distinct locations. Use Translink’s journey information for your rail or coach itinerary.

Before you travel

Grand Central Station taxi questions

Can I pre-book a taxi from Belfast Grand Central Station?

Yes. Enter the station as your pickup, choose your destination and confirm the passenger and suitcase counts to view a quote. You select the pickup date and time when you continue to book. Short-notice journeys may need availability approval before payment.

Where will my driver collect me?

Follow the collection point agreed for your booking. The public-hire taxi rank is separate from a pre-booked private transfer; do not assume your driver will be waiting there. If you do not have a confirmed meeting point, contact us on WhatsApp before making your way outside.

I'm arriving at Grand Central from Dublin — can you take me anywhere in Northern Ireland?

Yes. Enter Belfast Grand Central Station as the pickup and your destination anywhere in Northern Ireland, from the Causeway Coast to Fermanagh, for a fixed price before you book. Book for a realistic time after your Enterprise train or coach is due, allowing for baggage and the walk to the agreed collection point.

Can I get a taxi from Belfast City Airport to Grand Central Station?

Yes. Select George Best Belfast City Airport as the pickup and Belfast Grand Central Station as the destination for a fixed quote, then add your flight number when you book so we can monitor your landing. Airport pickups include up to 60 minutes complimentary waiting. The same journey can be booked the other way for a flight.

Can I get a taxi from Belfast International Airport to Grand Central Station?

Yes. Choose Belfast International Airport as the pickup and Belfast Grand Central Station as the destination to see your fixed price. Add your flight number when booking; we monitor inbound flights where possible and airport pickups include up to 60 minutes complimentary waiting.

Can I book a taxi to Belfast International Airport?

Yes. Select Belfast Grand Central Station as the pickup and Belfast International Airport as the destination. You can also book the reverse journey. Applicable airport access charges are shown through the existing quote system.

Can I travel to Dublin Airport?

Yes. Private transfers are available between Grand Central and Dublin Airport in either direction, subject to availability. Your fixed quote accounts for the applicable airport charges and tolls; this page does not publish an estimated fare.

Can I book a taxi to the station from Newtownabbey?

Yes. Enter your full Newtownabbey pickup address and select Belfast Grand Central Station as your destination. Choose your collection time later in the booking process, allowing time to reach your train or coach.

Can I book a return journey?

Yes. Select Return in the calculator. The existing 5% return-booking discount applies where an instant online price is offered, and you can review the combined fare before paying.

Can I pay securely online?

Yes. Eligible bookings use our secure online card checkout. If a short-notice journey needs availability approval, follow the payment instructions once it is approved. Keep your booking confirmation for your journey details.

What happens if my train is delayed?

Contact us on WhatsApp as soon as you know, giving your booking reference and revised arrival time. We will discuss what is possible; a revised pickup is subject to availability. Station pickups have the normal non-airport allowance of 10 minutes from the agreed pickup time. Further waiting may be charged. Automatic train monitoring and the airport waiting allowance are not included.

Can I book a 7-seater for a station transfer?

Yes. Choose up to seven passengers in the calculator when the 7 Seater Minibus is available. Enter your luggage accurately so we can assess the space required. Group journeys use the existing minibus pricing, minimum notice and availability rules; your booking must be confirmed before travel.

Is Business Class available for Grand Central Station?

Business Class is available to select when offered for your journey and passenger/luggage combination. The current capacity is up to three passengers and two large suitcases. It uses the existing Business Class fare and availability rules. Station collections use an agreed collection point; airport Meet & Greet and flight-monitoring benefits do not apply to a station pickup.

Can I book an Estate for extra luggage?

Yes. Enter your passenger and suitcase counts to see suitable vehicle options. Saloon and Estate bookings accommodate up to four passengers; the Estate offers additional luggage capacity. Its vehicle fare is £7 above the equivalent Saloon fare before the applicable return discount. Airport access charges remain separate from the vehicle supplement.
