## NFC tag position (installation — not printed on the plate)

**Option A (120 × 80 mm):** mount NTAG213 / NTAG215 behind the green TAP centre —
≈ **25.0 mm from the left trim edge**, **50.0 mm from the top trim edge**. See
`public/tap/print-120x80/nfc-install-guide-120x80.pdf`.

Do not place the chip under the QR white card. The passenger-facing print must **not**
show registration crosses or “NFC here” marks — use the install guide for fitters only.

## Program an NTAG213 or NTAG215

No subscription service is required. Use any NDEF writer app (e.g. NFC Tools) on a phone that supports NFC writing:

1. Erase / format the tag if it was used before.
2. Add an **NDEF URI** record (not plain text).
3. URI: `https://www.myairporttaxini.co.uk/tap`
4. Write to the tag and lock it (optional but recommended for headrest plates).
5. Test with an iPhone and an Android phone — both should open the same page as the QR code.

NTAG213 has enough memory for this short HTTPS URL. NTAG215 is fine if already stocked.

## Recommended materials and finishes

Suitable for a single physical proof, then a small vehicle run:

1. **Matte white or black PVC / vinyl sticker** (0.1–0.2 mm) with permanent adhesive — matte laminate to cut glare in the cabin. Laminate **over** the QR carefully; avoid soft-touch coatings that haze the code.
2. **Matte acrylic plate** (1–2 mm) — UV print or laminated print face, rounded corners, optional foam tape or screw posts on the headrest shell. Feels more premium; slightly thicker.
3. **Domed gel label** over a printed vinyl base — durable and tactile; confirm the dome does not blur the QR (keep dome clear and thin over the code, or leave QR undomed).

Finish: **matte** preferred (less reflection for phones). Colour: rich black / navy as artwork; emerald accents as designed. Ask the printer for a **single proof** at exact size, then scan the QR and tap the programmed NFC tag before bulk.
