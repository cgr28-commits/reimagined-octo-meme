Proposed shared design system

Navy #071C38 header and footer   Emerald #2FBF4A buttons and accents   White content area   #F5F7FA background

  • One layout file (shared/email-kit.ts) used by every customer email. It contains:
    • Header: the logo from the website, a small green label, and the title.
    • Body: greeting, a status banner, booking reference, a journey card (pickup → drop-off, date and time), detail rows, a price block, the green button, information panels, ‘Need help?’, and the footer.
  • Pending vs confirmed are clearly different. Pending requests get an amber ‘Awaiting confirmation — not yet confirmed’ banner and a ‘Not charged’ label. Only real confirmations get the green ‘Booking confirmed’ banner and ‘Paid in full’.
  • Empty fields are skipped. Rows with no value aren't shown, so there are no blank lines or ‘undefined’ (for example, a missing flight number).
  • Fonts: Manrope for text and Cormorant Garamond for prices, matching the website. Where web fonts are blocked (Gmail, Outlook), they fall back to Helvetica/Arial and Georgia.
  • Footer: My Airport Taxi NI · website · 028 9602 2952 · bookings@ · Terms, Cancellation Policy and Privacy. ‘Need help?’ shows WhatsApp as @belfasttaxi, linking to the website’s contact page, not the number. Your personal name and Rinkel don't appear.
  • Built to work in email apps:
    • Table layout, 600px wide, inline styles and a plain-text version.
    • Outlook-safe buttons, alt text on images and no JavaScript.
    • Works down to 320px wide.
    • Light dark-mode adjustments.
  • Wording, subjects, triggers, recipients, links and tracking stay the same. Only the layout around them changes. The existing regex clean-up passes get retired for emails moved to the kit.
