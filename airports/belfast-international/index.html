Taxi to Belfast International Airport

Check that your flight leaves from Belfast International at Aldergrove (BFS), rather than Belfast City Airport (BHD). Use your home, hotel or business address as the pickup and BFS as the destination. Allow around 30 minutes from Belfast city centre in normal traffic, plus extra road time at busy periods and the check-in time recommended by your airline. Your booking needs the time you want to be collected, not your flight departure time.

Pre-booked pickups at Aldergrove

For an arrival, put Belfast International Airport in Pickup location and enter your destination address. Add your flight number when completing the booking so we can monitor your flight. Free pickups are from the Long Stay car park, or choose Express Pick-Up beside the terminal and the airport's charge is included in your fixed price. Follow the collection instructions confirmed by your driver after you have collected your luggage. Business Class airport pickups include Meet & Greet with a name board; check the details for the vehicle and collection service you book.

Fixed-price quotes and airport access charges

Enter both addresses, passengers and suitcases, then choose “Get My Fixed Price”. Review the available vehicles and quote before adding your date, pickup time and contact details to book. Vehicle supplements form part of the journey fare. Applicable airport terminal access charges are separate from that fare in the price breakdown and included in the total you see. The 5% return-booking discount applies to the journey fare, not the airport access charges.

Belfast International Airport taxi FAQs

How much is a taxi to Belfast International Airport?
Every Belfast International Airport taxi is priced from the exact pickup address, so enter your street, passengers and suitcases in the quote box for your fixed fare. The price you are shown is the price you pay — it does not change with traffic, delays or the time of day.
Is the airport drop-off or pick-up charge included?
Yes. Your fixed quote includes the applicable Express Drop-Off or Express Pick-Up charge at Belfast International. Where the airport offers a designated free drop-off or pick-up area, the quote lets you choose that instead and the Express charge is removed from your total.
Where will my driver meet me at Belfast International Airport?
Free pickups are from the Long Stay car park. If you would rather be collected right beside the terminal, choose the Express Pick-Up option in the quote and the airport's charge is included in your fixed price. Your booking confirmation gives the exact collection point and your driver contacts you on the day. Business Class pickups include Meet & Greet with a name board in arrivals. Please follow the instructions for your booking rather than heading to the public taxi rank.
Is Belfast International the same as Belfast Airport?
Yes. When people say Belfast Airport they usually mean Belfast International Airport (BFS) at Aldergrove, about 30 minutes north-west of Belfast city centre. The smaller George Best Belfast City Airport (BHD) is beside the harbour. We run pre-booked, fixed-price taxis to and from both.
What happens if my flight into Aldergrove is delayed?
Add your flight number when you book. We monitor inbound flights where possible and adjust the collection time, and airport pickups include up to 60 minutes complimentary waiting time.
Can you take a family or group with lots of luggage?
Yes. Choose an Estate for extra suitcases or a 7 Seater Minibus when available for larger groups. Enter your passengers and suitcases in the quote so we send the right vehicle.
Do you cover early-morning flights from Belfast International?
Yes. Booking is online 24/7 and we run early-morning and late-night airport transfers. We will suggest a pickup time that leaves enough margin for the M2/M22 corridor and your airline's check-in.
How long is a taxi from Belfast International Airport to Belfast city centre?
Around 30 minutes in normal traffic, via the A57 and M2. Allow longer at morning and evening peak times. Your fixed price does not change if the journey takes longer.
Do I need to queue at the taxi rank?
No. Ours is a pre-booked private transfer, not the airport taxi rank. Your driver is booked for your flight and meets you at the collection point in your booking confirmation, so there is no queue and no meter.
Can I get a taxi from Belfast International to Dublin Airport or Derry?
Yes. We run long-distance transfers across Northern Ireland and the whole of Ireland, including Belfast International to Dublin Airport, City of Derry Airport and the North Coast. Enter both places in the quote box for a fixed price.
Belfast International or Belfast City Airport — which is which?
Belfast International (BFS, Aldergrove) is in County Antrim, north-west of the city, and handles most holiday flights. George Best Belfast City Airport (BHD) is beside the harbour, close to the city centre. Check your ticket for the airport code — we run fixed-price taxis to both.

Flying from the other Belfast airport? See our Belfast City Airport taxi guide.

Connecting with a train or coach?

Arrange a pre-booked transfer between Belfast International and Belfast Grand Central Station, in either direction. Read the station collection guidance and get a quote before booking.
